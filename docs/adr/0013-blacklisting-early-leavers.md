# 13. Blacklisting players who leave Legion TD early

Status: accepted (2026-10-31). Issue 29. Builds on ADR 0008 (the blacklist) and the replay parser (`infrastructure/replay`, `docs/research/replay-parser.md`).

## Context

In Legion TD a player who leaves early ruins the match for their team: their lane is left undefended for the rest of the game. Players asked for those leavers to be kept out of their next lobbies, which the blacklist and Auto-ban (ADR 0008) already do once a BattleTag is on the list. Adding them by hand after every match is tedious, and players rarely remember who left.

The game's menu socket says nothing about who left a match, but the game saves every match it plays as `LastReplay.w3g`, and the replay parser reads its players, leave records, chat and game clock. Result codes can't be trusted on custom maps: Legion TD reports an undocumented `0x0D` for everyone who quits, and at the end of a normal match everyone quits, so a result-code rule would flag the whole lobby. Leave **times** are reliable.

Two real replays shaped the rule. A forfeit (22 min): the West team voted `-ff` from 20:45 to 21:35, the game clock stopped at 21:36.5, and all eight players left between 21:42 and 21:58; nobody is a leaver. A game whose host had the wrong map version (8 min): one player left at 0:20 and the rest at 8:03. The owner decided that leavers can be blacklisted at any time: there is no grace period at the start and no minimum match length, so the 0:20 player counts.

A leaver can also get away by having the others end the game by vote: someone leaves in round 1 and the rest type `-draw` at once, so the end, and its final-round window, comes right after the leave.

## Decision

- **Opt-in switch.** *Blacklist players who leave Legion TD early*, in the Blacklist tab, off by default, saved in `friends.json` with the other blacklist settings (`blacklistLeavers`, written only when on). Nothing happens while it is off: no replay is looked for or read.
- **Legion TD only.** `[leavers] map` names the tracked map (`[[maps.tracked]]`, ADR 0012) whose matches count. A replay counts only when its map file name matches that map's pattern (`mapupdate.Match`), and only when the player's own BattleTag is one of its human players.
- **Reading the replay** (`contract.ReplayLocator`, `infrastructure/discovery`; `contract.ReplayReader`, `infrastructure/replay`). After every match the player played, hosted or joined (the follow engine's `inMatch` turning off), the companion looks every 2 seconds, for at most 2 minutes, for the most recently changed `<Documents or Application Support>/Warcraft III/BattleNet/<account>/Replays/LastReplay.w3g` (macOS: `~/Library/Application Support/Warcraft III/BattleNet` and `…/Blizzard/Warcraft III/BattleNet`; Windows: the Documents known folder, `%USERPROFILE%\Documents` and OneDrive's Documents; Linux: the Documents folders of every Wine or Proton prefix discovery knows). It reads it once it changed after the match began and its size and time are the same on two looks in a row. Each file (path, time and size) is read once. Access is read-only: the folders are listed, the file is read, nothing is written, moved or deleted.
- **The account folder is never shown.** The folder between `BattleNet` and `Replays` is the Battle.net account id. It is never logged, printed, shown or saved: the locator logs only whether it found a replay, the companion never logs the path, and a read error is logged without it.
- **When the match ended** (`follow.EarlyLeavers`, pure): when the game clock stopped (`dto.Replay.Ended`, from action `0x62`: the time the last clock value was first sent, once it was sent again with a rising repeat counter, as it is after a king dies or an end vote passes). When the replay doesn't show that (the saver left before the end), the end is when the remaining humans quit: the start of the last cluster of human leave records, leaves at most 30 seconds apart, when that cluster reaches the end of the recording; otherwise the game time, since the match went on after those leaves (one player leaving at 2:47 of a game recorded to 16:45 left early).
- **Rounds** (`dto.Replay.Rounds`, from action `0x62`). Legion TD's script runs a timer that every player's game reports with action `0x62`; at the start of each round it sends the same value again with a counter above 1. A round begins at the first such repeat after at least 30 seconds without one; the phase the end of the match opens is not a round. This is the map's own timing, not anything a player clicks. In real replays it matched the rounds of an 8-player game (7 rounds in 21 minutes) and of games against computers (3 and 4 rounds), and found 6 and 10 rounds in older 2.00 replays.
- **Who left early.** The final round is the last round that began before the end; when the replay shows no rounds it is the window `[leavers] final_round` (`4m`) before the end.
  - *Played out:* a human who left before the final round began. Anyone who left in the final round or later is never a leaver.
  - *Ended by vote:* an end vote is a human's chat line, in any channel (including team chat only the map saw), reading `-draw` or `-ff` (any case, spaces trimmed) in the final round; earlier ones, such as a joke `-draw` mid-game, change nothing. When there is one, the cut-off is the first end vote: whoever left before it is a leaver, even in the final round; whoever left at or after it is not.
  - Only human, non-observer players with a BattleTag and a leave record count. Each leaver gets the round they left in.
- **Never blacklisted** (`Engine.AddLeavers`): the player themself, anyone on their Battle.net friends list, anyone in their game team or follow group, players without a BattleTag (computers), and anyone already on the blacklist. Nobody is added unless the game is connected and has sent the whole friends list on this connection, so a friend can always be told apart. The blacklist's 500-player limit applies.
- **Reason and note.** Every automatic entry gets a plain-text reason on `dto.BlacklistEntry.Reason`, the owner's wording: "Left Legion TD early (round 3)", or "(minute 13)" when the replay shows no rounds (`follow.LeftEarly`). The entry's own date says when. The same line goes into the player's note (`notes.json`): as the note, or on a new line of an existing note when it fits in 500 characters; an existing note it doesn't fit is left alone. A player the player bans by hand in a lobby they host gets "You banned them in <lobby name>, <date>". Entries saved before reasons existed load without one. The window shows the reason under the name in the Blacklist tab and in the player tooltip, as text (`textContent`), never HTML.
- **Activity and log.** One Activity line per match: "Blacklisted Player#1234 for leaving Legion TD early (round 3)." or "Blacklisted 2 players for leaving Legion TD early." The log gets `leavers.added` with counts and how the match ended, `leavers.skipped` with a reason (another map, not the player's match), `leavers.no_replay` and `leavers.read_failed`; never BattleTags, chat, notes, reasons or the replay's path.

## Consequences

- A leaver is kept out of the player's next lobbies by Auto-ban without the player doing anything, and the reason says why they are on the list.
- A player who leaves on purpose in the first minutes, or when a host's wrong map version makes the game useless, is blacklisted too: the owner accepted that, and the player can remove them in the Blacklist tab.
- Rounds depend on Legion TD Team OZE's timer; a version that changes it falls back to the fixed final-round window, which is a guess at round length, until the round reading is checked again.
- The rule depends on the English `-draw` and `-ff` commands of Legion TD Team OZE; another map would need its own rule, which is why only the configured map counts.
- Replays are read only after a match the player played and only while the switch is on; the game's replay files are never changed. Windows and Linux replay folders follow the same layout as macOS's but haven't been checked on a real installation yet.
